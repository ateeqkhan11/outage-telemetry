Key terms of the pilot

Official callA service is down only when two independent companies' probes see it fail while their own connection works. One provider's glitch can't trigger a payout.
Signed recordEvery call is hash-chained and Ed25519-signed by the network that made it. Anyone can check it, in the browser or with the public keys.
History and reportsOfficial uptime, every outage against 1 h and 2 h waiting periods, per-network numbers, CSV and signed-record downloads.
APIThe same official calls and signed records, pulled into your own trigger checks.
PilotA handful of firms, free during the pilot, in exchange for feedback on what the data should cover.
